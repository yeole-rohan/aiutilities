3 Types of Prompts You'll Get

1

Concise prompt

A short, direct instruction — best when you need a quick answer and ChatGPT's general knowledge is sufficient. Works for well-defined, simple tasks.

2

Detailed prompt

Includes role, context, constraints, and output format. Gets significantly more specific and useful responses for complex tasks where nuance matters.

3

Chain-of-thought prompt

Asks ChatGPT to think through the problem step by step before answering. Dramatically improves accuracy for reasoning, analysis, math, and decision-making tasks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do these prompts work with GPT-4 and GPT-4o?
Yes. These prompts work with all ChatGPT versions (3.5, 4, 4o). Chain-of-thought prompts benefit more from GPT-4 and GPT-4o since they have stronger reasoning capabilities. For GPT-3.5, the concise and detailed options are more reliable.
Can I use these prompts with Claude or Gemini?
Yes — the prompting techniques (role assignment, context, chain-of-thought) apply universally to all large language models. For Claude and Gemini, the detailed and chain-of-thought prompts work especially well. See the AI Prompt Generator tool for prompts tuned to specific models.
