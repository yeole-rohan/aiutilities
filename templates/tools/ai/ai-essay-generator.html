Essay Types Explained

Argumentative Takes a clear stance and uses evidence to persuade the reader. Best for debates and opinion essays.
Expository Explains a topic objectively with facts. No personal opinion — best for informational essays.
Descriptive Paints a vivid picture using sensory details. Best for describing people, places, or events.
Narrative Tells a story with a beginning, middle, and end. Often uses first person. Best for personal essays.
Compare Examines similarities and differences between two subjects. Best for analytical writing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I submit this essay as my own work?
We recommend using generated essays as a starting point, study reference, or structural guide — not submitting them directly as academic work. Most educational institutions have policies against submitting AI-generated content without disclosure. Always check your institution's policy.
How accurate is the content?
The AI generates well-reasoned arguments but may not have the latest statistics or research. Always verify specific facts, dates, and citations before using them in academic work. Add real sources and citations yourself.
Can I get a longer essay?
Generate up to ~1,000 words per run. For longer essays, generate the introduction, each main body argument, and conclusion separately using specific prompts, then combine them. This gives you better quality than one very long generation.
Does it add citations and references?
The AI may reference studies or statistics in general terms but does not produce verified citations. Add real, verifiable references yourself using academic databases like Google Scholar, JSTOR, or PubMed.
