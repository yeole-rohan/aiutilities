Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work for both Excel and Google Sheets?
Yes — select your target application. Most formulas are identical, but some differ (e.g. XLOOKUP works in Excel 365 and Google Sheets but not older Excel; QUERY is Google Sheets-only; TEXTJOIN has different syntax). Selecting "Both" generates the most compatible version that works in both.
Can it help me understand a formula I didn't write?
Yes — paste the formula in the task field with the description "Explain what this formula does: [formula]". The AI will break down each function, what each argument does, and what the overall formula computes.
