Prompt Engineering Techniques Used

1

Role assignment

"Act as a senior marketing strategist..." — assigning a persona sets expertise level and response style.

2

Context + constraints

Providing background ("for a SaaS startup with 50 users") and constraints ("under 150 words") dramatically narrows the output to what you actually need.

3

Output format specification

Telling the AI exactly how to format the response ("in a numbered list with one-line rationale per item") prevents vague prose and ensures structured, usable output.
