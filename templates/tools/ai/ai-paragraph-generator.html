When to Use Each Tone

📖

Informative

Best for explainers, knowledge articles, and educational content. States facts clearly without opinion or persuasion.

🎯

Persuasive

Best for essays, opinion pieces, sales copy, and pitches. Builds argument and calls the reader to a specific view or action.

💬

Conversational

Best for blog posts, emails, and social copy. Feels like talking to a smart friend — approachable and direct.

🎓

Formal / Academic

Best for research papers, reports, and professional documents. Precise language, third-person voice, objective stance.
