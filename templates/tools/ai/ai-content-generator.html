Frequently Asked Questions

Can I publish the generated content directly?
Yes, but we recommend reviewing and personalizing it first. Add specific examples, statistics, personal insights, and brand voice. Google values original, helpful content — AI-generated drafts that you edit and enrich perform well.
Will this content rank on Google?
AI-generated content can rank if it is helpful, accurate, and provides genuine value. Add unique insights, internal links, and external references. Avoid publishing thin, generic AI content without editing — Google penalizes low-value pages.
How specific should my topic be?
Specific topics produce better content. "How to reduce customer churn in SaaS using onboarding emails" beats "customer retention". The more focused your topic, the more focused and useful the output.
What's the maximum content length?
Up to ~1,200 words per generation. For longer pieces, generate sections separately (introduction, each main section, conclusion) and assemble them. This also gives you more control over the structure.
