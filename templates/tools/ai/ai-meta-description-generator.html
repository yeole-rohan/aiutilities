Meta Description Best Practices

📏

Keep it 140–160 characters

Too short wastes space; too long gets cut off. Aim for 150 characters — our AI always stays within limit.

🎯

Include your primary keyword naturally

Google bolds the keyword in results, improving visibility. Don't keyword-stuff — one natural use is enough.

⚡

Use action verbs and a clear CTA

"Learn how to…", "Get your free…", "Try it now" — active language increases clicks versus passive descriptions.

✅

Make it unique for every page

Duplicate meta descriptions across pages confuse Google and reduce your CTR. Every page deserves its own.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do meta descriptions affect SEO rankings?
Meta descriptions are not a direct ranking factor in Google's algorithm. However, a compelling meta description improves your CTR, which is a behavioral signal that indirectly influences rankings over time.
Will Google always use my meta description?
Not always. Google may replace your meta description with a snippet from your page if it thinks that better matches the search query. To maximize use of your description, make it highly relevant to your page's main topic.
Should I use all 3 generated options?
Only one goes on your page at a time. Use all 3 as A/B test variants if you have a testing setup. Otherwise, pick the one that best matches your page's tone and most important value proposition.
How do I add the meta description to my site?
Add it to your page's <head> as: <meta name="description" content="Your description here">. In WordPress use Yoast SEO or RankMath. In Shopify edit the SEO section. In Webflow use the Page Settings panel.
