Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI summarizer?
Very accurate for factual text. The AI (Llama 3.3 70B) is trained to extract only information present in the original text. It does not add or fabricate information. Always verify important facts from the original source.
Can I summarize a PDF?
Yes — copy the text from your PDF and paste it here. For PDFs with selectable text, use Ctrl+A in your PDF viewer to select all. For scanned PDFs, use our OCR PDF tool first to extract the text.
What is the text limit?
Up to 12,000 characters (~1,800–2,000 words) per summary. For longer documents, split them into sections and summarize each separately, then combine.
Which output format should I choose?
Use Bullet Points for quick scanning and presentations. Use Paragraph for sharing the summary in emails or reports. Use TLDR + Key Takeaways when you need a quick one-liner plus main points.
