What Makes Great Rap Lyrics

🎤

Multi-syllable rhymes

Single-syllable end rhymes (cat/bat) are basic. Great rappers rhyme across multiple syllables — "motivating" with "oscillating". The more syllables in a rhyme, the more impressive.

⚡

Internal rhymes

End-line rhymes are expected. Internal rhymes (rhyming words mid-line) create a dense, layered sound that rewards repeated listening. This is what separates bars from poetry.

📖

Specific details

Vague lyrics about "the streets" feel generic. Specific details — a brand, a street name, a smell, a year — make the listener feel like they were there. Specificity = authenticity.

🎯

Punchlines

A punchline lands at the end of a couplet — a twist, a double meaning, or a clever metaphor that makes the listener rewind. Build up to it; don't bury it mid-bar.
