Newsletter Best Practices

📧

Subject line is everything

47% of recipients decide whether to open based on the subject line alone. The AI generates a subject line and preview text — test multiple options if your platform supports it.

✂️

Short paragraphs

Email readers scan, they don't read. Keep paragraphs to 2–3 sentences. Use subheadings to let readers jump to what interests them. The AI structures output for scanners.

🎯

One CTA per email

Multiple CTAs split attention and reduce clicks on each. Pick one primary action — read an article, buy a product, book a call — and everything in the email should point toward it.

📅

Consistency beats perfection

A good newsletter sent every week beats a perfect newsletter sent whenever inspiration strikes. Use AI to maintain your publishing cadence even on low-inspiration weeks.
