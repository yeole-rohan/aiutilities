Frequently Asked Questions

Are the answers always correct?
The AI generates factually grounded content, but for high-stakes educational use (exams, certifications) always verify the answers against authoritative sources. For general knowledge, trivia, and revision practice the accuracy is very high — but a quick review before use is recommended, especially for niche or highly technical topics.
Can I export the quiz to Google Forms or Kahoot?
The quiz is generated as plain text that you can copy and paste into any platform — Google Forms, Kahoot, Quizlet, Typeform, or a Word document. The structured format (question, options A-D, answer) makes it easy to transfer to any quiz tool.
