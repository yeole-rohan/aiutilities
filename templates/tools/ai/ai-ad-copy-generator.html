What Good Ad Copy Does

🎯

Leads with benefit

"Project tracking made simple" beats "Our software has a Gantt chart". Customers buy outcomes, not features. Lead with what they get, not what you built.

⚡

Creates urgency

Deadlines, limited availability, and fear of missing out drive clicks. "Limited spots", "offer ends Friday", "join 10,000+ businesses" — all create pressure to act now.

🔢

Uses numbers

Numbers are more credible than adjectives. "Save 3 hours per week" beats "saves lots of time". "Trusted by 50,000 users" beats "popular with businesses".

📏

Respects character limits

Google Ads: 30 chars per headline, 90 per description. Meta: 125 chars primary text shown before truncation. The AI formats to platform limits so copy is never cut off.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many ad variants should I test?
Start with 3 variants per ad group (this tool generates exactly 3). Vary one element between each — headline, CTA, or benefit focus. Let them run until you have 100+ impressions each, then cut the worst performer and replace it with a new variant based on what the winner taught you.
Can I use this for product listings on Amazon or Etsy?
The Meta/TikTok formats work well for organic social posts. For Amazon and Etsy listings, try the Product Description Generator or Etsy Product Description Generator — they're specifically optimised for marketplace listing formats and SEO.
