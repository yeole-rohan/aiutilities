What Makes a Great Short Story?

1

Hook in the first line

The opening sentence must create immediate intrigue. A question, a shocking statement, or an action already in progress — anything but backstory or description.

2

One central conflict

Short stories only have space for one main problem. Every scene should relate to it. Resist the urge to add subplots — focus creates tension.

3

A resonant ending

The ending doesn't have to be happy, but it must feel earned and inevitable in hindsight. The best endings reframe what came before.
