Frequently Asked Questions

Is this aligned to Common Core or other standards?
The lesson plans follow general best-practice structure and can reference Common Core or other standards when you include the standard code in your subject description (e.g. "CCSS.MATH.CONTENT.5.NF.A.1 — adding fractions"). Without a standard code, the AI structures objectives using broadly accepted pedagogical frameworks.
Can I customise the generated plan?
Yes — copy the plan and paste it into Google Docs, Word, or your school's lesson planning template. The AI provides the skeleton; your professional judgment about your specific students, classroom, and available resources will always improve it.
