Why Add FAQs to Your Content?

🔍

Google Featured Snippets

FAQs with concise 2–4 sentence answers are prime candidates for Google's "People Also Ask" boxes — free organic visibility above regular results.

📊

Dwell Time & Engagement

FAQs answer visitors' next questions before they bounce. Keeping users on your page longer is a strong positive signal to Google's ranking algorithm.

⚡

Voice Search Ranking

Voice assistants love FAQ-style content. Questions phrased naturally ("How do I…", "What is…") match how people speak to Siri, Alexa, and Google Assistant.

🛒

Conversion & Trust

FAQs reduce objections and build trust on product and service pages. Answering "Is this safe?" or "What's the refund policy?" directly increases conversion rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add FAQPage schema to get rich results?
Add JSON-LD structured data to your page: {"@context": "https://schema.org", "@type": "FAQPage", "mainEntity": [...]}. Each FAQ becomes a Question entity with an acceptedAnswer. Google Search Console shows if your FAQs are eligible for rich results in the Enhancements report.
How many FAQs should I include on a page?
5–10 FAQs is the sweet spot for most pages. Enough to cover real user questions without overwhelming the reader. For pillar content or comparison pages, 12–15 can work. Prioritize quality — each answer should be genuinely helpful, not padded filler.
Where should I place FAQs on my page?
For SEO, place FAQs near the bottom of the page — after your main content — so they address questions that arise after reading. For product pages, place FAQs directly below the product description to handle purchase objections immediately.
