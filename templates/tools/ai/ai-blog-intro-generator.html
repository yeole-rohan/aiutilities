Anatomy of a Great Blog Introduction

1

The hook

The first sentence must stop the reader. Use a bold claim, a surprising statistic, a relatable frustration, or a provocative question. Never start with "In today's world…" or a dictionary definition.

2

Agitate the problem

Briefly describe the problem your reader is facing in terms they'd use themselves. This creates an "they get me" moment that builds trust and keeps them reading.

3

The promise

Tell the reader exactly what they'll get by reading to the end. A specific, concrete outcome ("by the end of this post you'll have a 3-step system") is far more compelling than a vague teaser.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should a blog introduction be?
Aim for 100–200 words. Long enough to hook the reader and state your promise, short enough to get to the actual content quickly. Readers decide in the first few lines whether to keep reading — every word in the intro must earn its place. This tool generates introductions in that optimal range.
Should I include my target keyword in the introduction?
Yes — including your primary keyword naturally in the first 100 words is a standard SEO best practice. It signals to search engines what the page is about.
