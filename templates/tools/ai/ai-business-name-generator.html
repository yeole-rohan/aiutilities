What Makes a Great Business Name?

🎯

Easy to Spell & Say

If people can't spell it after hearing it, they can't Google it. Test by saying it out loud and asking someone to write it down.

🌐

.com Domain Available

Check availability immediately. Use Namecheap, GoDaddy, or Google Domains. If your .com is taken, consider a suffix (get + name, try + name) before settling for .co or .io.

📱

Social Handle Available

Check Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and TikTok for your shortlisted names using Namecheckr. Consistency across platforms is important for brand recognition.

™

No Trademark Conflicts

Before registering, do a basic trademark search in your country (USPTO for US, EUIPO for EU). A name may be free on Google but trademarked in your industry class.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check if a business name is taken?
Check: (1) Domain availability at any domain registrar, (2) Trademark database for your country, (3) Your state/country's business registration database, (4) Google the name to see existing businesses. All four checks are needed before committing.
Should I use my own name as the business name?
Eponymous names work well for consultants, freelancers, and personal brands (accountants, lawyers, designers). They're harder to scale and sell later since the business is tied to your identity. For scalable businesses or SaaS, a distinct brand name usually works better.
Is a shorter business name always better?
Shorter is easier to remember and type, but it's not everything. A slightly longer, memorable name beats a short, forgettable one. The sweet spot is 2 syllables or 2 short words. Some of the world's most valuable brands — Amazon, Apple, Google — are 2–3 syllables.
