Frequently Asked Questions

Can I edit the generated email?
Yes. Copy the email and paste it into your email client. Personalize it with specific names, dates, or details before sending. The AI gives you a solid first draft that you refine.
Does it include a subject line?
Yes, always. Every generated email includes a Subject: line at the top, followed by the greeting, body paragraphs, and a professional sign-off.
How specific should my description be?
The more detail you provide, the more relevant the email. Include: who it's for, what you're asking or communicating, any deadlines or context. A 2–3 sentence description produces the best results.
Is it safe to use for confidential business emails?
Your input is sent to Groq's API for processing and is not stored by us. For highly sensitive business information, avoid including confidential data in the context field.
