Question Types Explained

📖

Comprehension

Test understanding of a topic — what, who, when, where, how. Best for checking whether someone has understood core concepts and facts.

💬

Discussion

Open-ended prompts that require opinion, analysis, and evidence. "To what extent...", "Discuss the implications of...", "Compare and contrast..." — no single right answer.

🤝

Interview

Behavioural questions ("Tell me about a time when...") and situational questions ("How would you handle...") that reveal character, skills, and past behaviour.

📝

Exam / Assessment

Short-answer and structured questions suitable for graded assessment. Mix of recall, application, and analysis questions across Bloom's taxonomy levels.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between this and the AI Quiz Generator?
The AI Quiz Generator creates multiple-choice and true/false questions with answer keys — structured quiz format. This Question Generator creates open-ended, discussion, or interview questions that require longer responses. Use the Quiz Generator for assessment; use this for discussion, preparation, or research prompts.
Can I use these for interview prep?
Yes — select "Interview (Behavioural)" and enter the job role or skill as the topic (e.g. "product manager", "data analyst interview", "leadership and management"). You'll get behavioural and situational questions to practice with. Use the AI Answer Generator to then draft STAR-format answers.
