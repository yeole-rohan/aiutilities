When to Send a Thank You Email

💼

After a Job Interview

Send within 24 hours. Reference a specific part of the conversation to show you were engaged. It reinforces your interest and keeps you top of mind during deliberation.

🤝

After a Meeting or Call

Summarize what was agreed on, thank them for their time, and confirm next steps. Turns a conversation into a paper trail and shows reliability.

🎁

For a Gift or Favor

Be specific about what you appreciated and how it impacted you. Generic "thanks for everything" emails read as afterthoughts. Specificity makes appreciation feel real.

🔗

After a Referral or Introduction

Thank the person who made the introduction, and update them on how it went. This closes the loop and makes them more likely to help you again in the future.

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon should I send a thank you email after an interview?
Within 24 hours — ideally same day or the next morning. Sending within a few hours of the interview signals enthusiasm and responsiveness. Anything beyond 48 hours loses impact. If you interviewed with multiple people, send individual, personalized notes to each.
How long should a thank you email be?
3–5 sentences for most situations. Thank them, reference something specific, and close with a forward-looking line. For post-interview emails, 100–150 words is the sweet spot. Longer emails risk burying the gratitude in filler.
Should I send a thank you email or a handwritten card?
For professional contexts (interviews, business meetings), email is standard and expected — it's faster and creates a record. For personal contexts (gifts, major personal favors), a handwritten card adds a level of care that email can't match. When in doubt, do both: email immediately, card to follow.
