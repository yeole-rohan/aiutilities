Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the answers?
The AI is highly accurate on well-established topics in science, history, business, and general knowledge. It performs best on conceptual questions with broadly accepted answers. For rapidly changing topics (recent news, current prices, live data), verify answers with up-to-date sources. For medical, legal, or financial decisions, always consult a qualified professional.
What's the difference between this and the AI Summarizer?
The AI Summarizer condenses existing text you provide into a shorter form. This Answer Generator draws on its knowledge to answer questions — you don't provide text, you ask questions. Use the Summarizer when you have content to shorten; use this tool when you want the AI to explain or answer something from its own knowledge.
