What Makes an Effective Image Prompt

1

Be specific, not vague

"A forest" gets a generic result. "An ancient redwood forest in morning fog, shafts of golden light filtering through the canopy, ground covered in ferns" gets a stunning image. Specificity is everything.

2

Add lighting and camera details

Lighting transforms an image. "Golden hour backlight", "studio lighting with soft box", "neon reflections" — and camera terms like "wide angle", "macro", "bokeh" tell the AI how to frame the shot.

3

Reference artistic styles

Reference a photographer's style (Annie Leibovitz), a painting movement (Baroque, Impressionism), or a film (Blade Runner aesthetic). Style references dramatically shift the feel of the output.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the --ar and --v parameters in Midjourney prompts?
--ar sets the aspect ratio (e.g. --ar 16:9 for widescreen, --ar 1:1 for square). --v sets the model version (--v 6 is currently the latest). --style raw disables Midjourney's aesthetic enhancements for a more literal interpretation. The AI includes these parameters in generated Midjourney prompts.
Can I use these prompts in Canva AI or Adobe Firefly?
Yes — the descriptive prompts work across all text-to-image platforms. Midjourney-specific parameters (--ar, --v) should be removed when using other platforms since they use different syntax. Select "Any Image AI" for platform-agnostic prompts that work everywhere.
