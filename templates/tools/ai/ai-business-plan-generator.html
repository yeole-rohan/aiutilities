Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use this for a real investor pitch?
Yes — as a starting point. Use the AI output as a structured first draft, then refine each section with real market research, actual financial projections, and your specific competitive insights. No AI-generated plan should go to investors without human review and real data.
How long is the generated business plan?
The generated plan is typically 600–900 words across 6–8 sections. It's a high-level strategic outline — enough for an initial pitch or loan application — not a 40-page detailed business plan. You can always expand sections by re-generating with more specific inputs.
What if my idea is still vague?
That's fine — describe what you know. Even "an app that connects freelance designers with startups" gives enough for a solid structure. The more specific you are (who it's for, how you make money), the more actionable the output. You can iterate by regenerating with more detail.
