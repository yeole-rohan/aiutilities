What's in a Winning Proposal

1

Executive summary

Decision-makers often read only this. Summarise the problem, your solution, why you're the right choice, and the investment required — in under 200 words.

2

Specific deliverables

Vague scope leads to scope creep and disputes. List exactly what you'll deliver, what format it's in, and what's explicitly excluded. Clarity wins trust.

3

Clear next steps

End every proposal with a specific call to action — "sign and return by [date]", "schedule a 30-minute call to discuss". A proposal without a next step is an invitation to procrastinate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add my own pricing to the proposal?
Yes — the generated proposal includes a pricing section placeholder that you fill in with your actual rates. You can copy the proposal into a Google Doc or Word and add your specific figures, payment terms, and any required legal language before sending.
How is this different from the AI Business Plan Generator?
A business plan is an internal strategic document describing your entire business — market analysis, financials, operations. A proposal is an external document sent to a specific client to win a specific project or contract. Different purpose, different structure, different audience.
