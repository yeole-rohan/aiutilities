Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need the password to decrypt? ▼
Yes — this tool uses the correct password to open and re-save the PDF without encryption. It does not crack or bypass passwords. You must already know the password.
Are my files and password private? ▼
Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser using pdf-lib. Your PDF file and password are never sent to any server.
What if I get "wrong password"? ▼
The password you entered doesn't match the file's user password. Check caps lock, try alternative passwords you may have set, or contact whoever created the PDF.
