How to Password-Protect a PDF

Drop or click to select a PDF file. The file stays in your browser — it's never uploaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

What encryption does this tool use? ▼
pdf-lib uses AES-128 encryption (PDF 1.6 standard). This is compatible with all modern PDF readers including Adobe Acrobat, Preview, and most browsers.
Can I recover a forgotten password? ▼
No — there is no password recovery. If you forget the password, the PDF cannot be opened. Always keep a record of your passwords and the unencrypted original.
Are my files and passwords private? ▼
Yes — all processing happens in your browser. Your PDF and passwords are never sent to any server. The password is only used locally by pdf-lib to encrypt the file.
