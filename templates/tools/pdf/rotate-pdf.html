Frequently Asked Questions

Does rotation affect image quality? ▼
No — PDF rotation is a metadata operation. pdf-lib sets the page rotation angle without re-encoding content, so there's no quality loss.
Are my files private? ▼
Yes. All processing runs in your browser. Files are never uploaded to any server.
Can I undo a rotation? ▼
Yes — re-upload the rotated PDF and apply the opposite rotation: rotate 90° again to undo a 270° rotation, or rotate 180° twice to undo a 180° rotation.
