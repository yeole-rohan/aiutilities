Frequently Asked Questions

How much will it compress? ▼
Results vary by file. Text-heavy PDFs can see 20–40% reduction. Image-heavy PDFs may see less since pdf-lib does not re-encode embedded JPEG/PNG images. For aggressive image compression, use Ghostscript.
Will quality be affected? ▼
This tool uses lossless compression — it optimizes PDF structure without degrading text, vector graphics, or embedded image quality.
Are my files private? ▼
Yes — everything runs in your browser. Files are never uploaded to any server.
