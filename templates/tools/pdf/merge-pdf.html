Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server? ▼
No. All processing uses pdf-lib running entirely in your browser. Files stay on your device — nothing is uploaded to any server.
What if my PDF is password-protected? ▼
Password-protected PDFs cannot be merged without decrypting them first. Use the Decrypt PDF tool to remove the password, then merge.
Is there a file size limit? ▼
No hard limit — but very large PDFs (100MB+) may be slow to process depending on your device memory. For best performance, merge in smaller batches.
