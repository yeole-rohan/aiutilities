Full PDF to Word Converters (Free)

For full layout-preserving DOCX conversion — including tables, images, and formatting — use one of these trusted free tools:

Google Docs

Upload the PDF to Google Drive, open with Google Docs, then download as DOCX. Works well for most text-based PDFs. Free with a Google account.

Microsoft Word

Open the PDF directly in Microsoft Word 2013 or later — it automatically converts to editable DOCX. Works best with text-based PDFs.

LibreOffice Writer

Free, open-source desktop software. Open a PDF with the Draw extension, then export as DOCX. Handles complex layouts better than web converters.

Smallpdf / ILovePDF

Free online converters with decent layout preservation. Files are uploaded to their servers — check their privacy policy before converting sensitive documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why doesn't my scanned PDF extract any text? ▼
Scanned PDFs are images of documents — they contain no actual text characters, only pixels. Extracting text requires OCR (Optical Character Recognition). Use Google Docs (which has built-in OCR) or Adobe Acrobat's OCR feature.
Will the formatting be preserved? ▼
The text extractor returns plain text only — no tables, columns, images, or formatting. For formatting, use Google Docs or Microsoft Word to open the PDF directly.
Are my files private? ▼
The text extractor on this page runs entirely in your browser — files are never uploaded. Third-party converters (Smallpdf, ILovePDF) do upload your file to their servers.
