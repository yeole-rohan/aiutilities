Frequently Asked Questions

How many images can I convert at once? ▼
There is no hard limit, but very large batches (50+ high-resolution images) may be slow on mobile devices due to browser memory constraints. For large batches, consider splitting into multiple conversions.
Will image quality be reduced? ▼
Images are embedded into the PDF at their original resolution. The PDF file size will be similar to or slightly larger than the combined source images.
Are my photos uploaded to a server? ▼
No. All processing is done entirely in your browser using the jsPDF library. Your images never leave your device.
