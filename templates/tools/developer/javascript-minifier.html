Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between minification and uglification? ▼
Minification removes whitespace and comments. Uglification (mangling) also renames variables to single letters — this is what Terser and webpack do for maximum compression.
Will this minifier rename my variables? ▼
No — this is a whitespace-and-comment stripper, not a full AST-based minifier. Variable names are preserved. Use Terser or esbuild for production builds.
What are license comments? ▼
Comments starting with /*! are conventionally used for licence notices (e.g. MIT licence headers). Tools like UglifyJS and this minifier preserve them even when stripping other comments.
