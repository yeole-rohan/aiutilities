Frequently Asked Questions

What is a cron job? ▼
A cron job is a scheduled task that runs automatically at specified times on a Unix-like server. Cron expressions define the schedule using 5 fields: minute, hour, day-of-month, month, and day-of-week.
What does */5 mean in a cron field? ▼
*/5 means "every 5 units". In the minute field, */5 means the job runs at minutes 0, 5, 10, 15 ... 55 — i.e. every 5 minutes.
Can both day-of-month and day-of-week be set? ▼
When both are specified (not *), most cron implementations run the job when either condition is true. To avoid confusion, set only one and leave the other as *.
What timezone does cron use? ▼
By default, cron uses the server's local timezone. This tool shows next run times in your browser's local timezone.
