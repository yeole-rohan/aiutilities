Frequently Asked Questions

How does DST (Daylight Saving Time) affect conversion? ▼
This converter uses the browser's Intl API which accounts for DST automatically. If a timezone observes DST, the offset shifts by +1 hour during summer months. The result always reflects the correct local time for the chosen date.
What is UTC? ▼
UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the global time standard. All other timezones are expressed as offsets from UTC (e.g. UTC+5:30 for India, UTC-5 for US Eastern). UTC never observes daylight saving time.
