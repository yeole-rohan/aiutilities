Frequently Asked Questions

Does the calculator include the start or end date? ▼
The difference calculation does not count either endpoint — it measures the gap between them. For example, Jan 1 to Jan 3 is 2 days. If you need to include both endpoints, add 1 to the result.
How are months calculated? ▼
The months value is an approximation — it divides the total days by 30.44 (average days per month). For exact month counts, use a calendar-aware approach since months vary in length.
Can I calculate working days only? ▼
This calculator shows calendar days (including weekends and holidays). For business-day calculations, multiply calendar days by approximately 5/7 as a rough estimate, or use a dedicated business-days tool.
