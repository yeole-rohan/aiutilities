Frequently Asked Questions

Are the start and end dates included? ▼
The calculator counts business days from the start date up to and including the end date (inclusive). If both dates are the same business day, the result is 1.
Which US holidays are excluded? ▼
The tool uses a fixed list of 10 US federal holidays: New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas. Floating holidays are approximated.
What if my country has different holidays? ▼
Uncheck "Exclude US federal holidays" to get the count based on weekends only — then subtract your country's holidays manually. This tool currently supports US holidays only.
