Frequently Asked Questions

How is age calculated? ▼
The tool subtracts the birth date from the target date, adjusting for months and days that have not fully elapsed so the result is always exact.
Can I calculate age on a future date? ▼
Yes — change the "Calculate age as of" date to any future date and the calculator will show the age on that day.
Does it account for leap years? ▼
Yes — the calculation uses actual calendar dates so leap years (366 days) are handled correctly.
