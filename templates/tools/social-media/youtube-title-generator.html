Frequently Asked Questions

How long should a YouTube title be?
Aim for 50–70 characters. YouTube shows 60–70 characters before truncating in most views. Put your most important keywords and hook in the first 50 characters. The full title can be up to 100 characters, but the visible portion is what drives clicks.
Should the keyword appear at the beginning of the title?
YouTube's algorithm gives more weight to keywords in the first few words. However, click-through rate (CTR) also matters. Balance SEO (keyword early) with click appeal (hook first). Test both approaches — sometimes a curiosity-driven hook that pushes the keyword to mid-title outperforms.
Do I need capital letters and brackets in titles?
Title Case vs. sentence case is a channel style choice — both work. Brackets like [2025], [Full Guide], or [MUST WATCH] can boost CTR by adding context and urgency, especially in search results. Use them sparingly — if every video has brackets, they lose effect.
Can I change my YouTube title after publishing?
Yes, you can update titles at any time in YouTube Studio. This is actually a useful strategy — if a video isn't getting views, try A/B-testing a new title. YouTube's built-in A/B testing for titles (via YouTube Studio Experiments) is available to eligible channels.
