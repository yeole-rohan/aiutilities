Will the bio fit Instagram's character limit?
Yes. Instagram allows 150 characters for the bio. Our AI is specifically instructed to keep every option under this limit. Emojis count as characters, which is accounted for.
Should I use line breaks in my bio?
Yes — line breaks make bios more scannable. In Instagram, you can add line breaks by pressing Enter in the bio editor on mobile. Some bios are generated as one-liners; you can manually format them with line breaks for better readability.
