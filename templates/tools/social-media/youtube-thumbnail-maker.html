Frequently Asked Questions

What size should a YouTube thumbnail be? ▼
YouTube recommends 1280×720 pixels (16:9 aspect ratio) with a minimum width of 640 pixels. The maximum file size is 2 MB. JPG, PNG, GIF, and WebP are accepted formats.
What makes a good YouTube thumbnail? ▼
High contrast colors, large readable text, and a clear focal point. Thumbnails are often viewed at small sizes, so avoid clutter. A face with a strong expression and bold text typically increases click-through rate.
Should I use JPG or PNG for thumbnails? ▼
JPG produces smaller file sizes and is fine for most thumbnails. Use PNG if you need lossless quality or transparency. YouTube accepts both — just stay under the 2 MB limit.
