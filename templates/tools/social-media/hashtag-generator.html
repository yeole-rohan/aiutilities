Frequently Asked Questions

How many hashtags should I use on Instagram?
Instagram allows up to 30. A 2023 study found 20–25 relevant hashtags produce the highest reach for most accounts. Focus on relevance over quantity — irrelevant hashtags hurt your reach.
Are the hashtags always relevant to my content?
The more specific your topic description, the more relevant the hashtags. Include your niche for best results. Always review the list and remove any that don't fit your specific post.
Should I use the same hashtags every post?
No. Instagram may suppress reach for accounts that use the exact same hashtag set repeatedly. Generate fresh hashtags for each post or rotate between several sets. Vary 10–15 tags each time.
Can I copy all 30 hashtags at once?
Yes — click the Copy button in the result box to copy all hashtags to your clipboard at once. Then paste them into your post.
