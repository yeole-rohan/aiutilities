What Drives Tweet Engagement

🎯

Bold opening

Twitter's feed is brutal — you have 0.3 seconds to stop a scroll. Start with a strong opinion, counterintuitive claim, or surprising stat.

💬

Ask a question

Tweets ending in a genuine question drive far more replies. Replies boost the tweet in the algorithm more than likes do.

🧵

Thread for depth

Threads outperform single tweets for complex ideas. Hook on tweet 1, deliver value through the thread, close with a CTA or summary.

⏰

Timing matters

Peak X/Twitter engagement is typically 8–10 AM and 6–9 PM in your audience's time zone on weekdays. Schedule accordingly.
