Frequently Asked Questions

Can I generate multiple versions of the same post?
Yes — submit the form multiple times for different variations. Change the tone or goal for variety. Use different versions for A/B testing or scheduling across a week.
Does it include emojis?
Yes for Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok — emojis are used strategically. LinkedIn and Twitter get fewer emojis to match the more professional/concise format of those platforms.
How specific should I be about the topic?
The more detail you give, the better the post. Include: what you're announcing or discussing, who it's for, what makes it special, and any offer or CTA. Vague inputs produce generic posts.
