Frequently Asked Questions

What is LinkedIn's headline character limit?
LinkedIn allows up to 220 characters for your headline. All generated headlines stay within this limit. LinkedIn will truncate your headline in search results after ~120 characters, so our AI leads with the most important information first.
Should I use the | separator?
The | (pipe) separator is the most common LinkedIn headline formatting convention. It visually separates your role, skills, and value prop. It's widely used and makes headlines scannable at a glance.
How often should I update my headline?
Update it when your role, goals, or target audience changes. Also test different versions — LinkedIn's SSI (Social Selling Index) improves with active profiles. You can generate fresh options here anytime for free.
