What Makes a Great Instagram Caption?

1

Strong first line

Instagram truncates captions after the first 2–3 lines — so your opening line does all the heavy lifting. Lead with the most compelling hook to trigger the "more" tap.

2

Clear call to action

Posts with a direct CTA ("tag a friend who needs this", "save for later") drive 2–3x more engagement than posts without one. Place it near the end, not the beginning.

3

Relevant hashtags

3–10 targeted hashtags outperform 30 generic ones. Mix niche-specific tags (#coffeeshopvibes) with broader ones (#morningroutine) to maximise discoverability without looking spammy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hashtags should I use?
Instagram recommends 3–5 hashtags per post as of 2024. The algorithm shift means fewer, more relevant hashtags now outperform the old strategy of maxing out at 30. This tool generates targeted, relevant hashtags — trim or add based on your niche.
Can I use these captions for Reels and Stories?
Yes — generated captions work for feed posts, Reels, and carousels. For Stories, adapt the caption as on-screen text or a caption overlay since Stories don't display a text caption field. For Reels, concise captions tend to perform better than long ones.
