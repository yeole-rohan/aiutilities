How to Check Color Contrast

Use the color pickers or type hex codes for your text and background colors. The preview updates instantly.
2
Check the contrast ratio
The ratio is calculated using the WCAG 2.1 luminance formula. Higher is better — 1:1 is no contrast (same color), 21:1 is maximum (black on white).
3
See compliance badges
Green badges indicate the combination passes WCAG AA or AAA for normal and large text. Adjust colors until you pass the required level.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which WCAG level do I need to meet? ▼
WCAG AA is the standard for most web projects and is legally required under WCAG-referenced laws (ADA, EAA, EN 301 549). AAA is the enhanced level — aim for it when possible but it's not always achievable for all text.
What counts as "large text"? ▼
Large text is defined as 18pt (24px) or 14pt (18.67px) bold. Headings typically qualify as large text. Normal body copy must meet the stricter 4.5:1 ratio.
Does contrast matter for images? ▼
WCAG 1.4.3 covers text — including text over images. If text is placed on a photo, the contrast must still meet requirements. Use a solid color overlay or text shadow to ensure compliance.
