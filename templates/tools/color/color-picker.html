Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between HEX, RGB, and HSL? ▼
HEX (#6366f1) is the web standard for CSS colours. RGB(99, 102, 241) defines colours by red, green, and blue intensity. HSL(239, 83%, 66%) defines by hue, saturation, and lightness — more intuitive for designers.
What are tints and shades? ▼
Tints are progressively lighter versions of a colour (mixed with white). Shades are progressively darker versions (mixed with black). They are used to build colour scales in design systems.
What is a complementary colour? ▼
The complementary colour sits directly opposite on the colour wheel (hue + 180 degrees). Complementary pairs create high contrast and vibrant visuals.
What is a triadic colour scheme? ▼
Triadic colours are three colours evenly spaced around the colour wheel (120 degrees apart). They create a balanced, vibrant palette.
