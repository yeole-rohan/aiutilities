Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between linear and radial gradients? ▼
A linear gradient transitions colors along a straight line at a given angle. A radial gradient radiates from a center point outward. Both support multiple color stops.
How do I add a gradient to a background in CSS? ▼
Use the background or background-image property with the generated value. Example: background: linear-gradient(135deg, #6366f1 0%, #ec4899 100%);
Can I use gradients on text? ▼
Yes — apply the gradient as a background on the text element, then add -webkit-background-clip: text; -webkit-text-fill-color: transparent; background-clip: text; to clip it to the text shape.
