Frequently Asked Questions

What is a complementary color palette? ▼
Complementary colors sit opposite each other on the color wheel (180° apart). They create high contrast and visual pop — great for call-to-action buttons or accent highlights.
What is a triadic palette? ▼
A triadic palette uses three colors equally spaced on the color wheel (120° apart). It offers balance and variety — commonly used in children's media and vibrant brand identities.
What is a monochromatic palette? ▼
A monochromatic palette uses shades and tints of a single hue. It is elegant and cohesive — ideal for minimal, professional, or corporate design where you want a clean look without loud contrasts.
