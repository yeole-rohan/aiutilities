Frequently Asked Questions

Can I paste a URL instead of article text?
This tool works with pasted text only. To use a URL, open the article in your browser, select all text (Ctrl+A), copy it, and paste it here. This avoids paywalls and gives the AI the full article content.
How long can the article be?
The tool handles up to approximately 10,000 words (about 12,000 characters). For longer documents like full reports or white papers, consider splitting them into sections and summarizing each separately.
Will the summary add opinions or change facts?
The AI is instructed to stay strictly factual — preserving names, numbers, dates, and quotes from the original. Always verify critical facts against the source, especially for news that will be shared or published.
