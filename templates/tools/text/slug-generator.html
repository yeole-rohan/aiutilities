What Makes a Good URL Slug?

1

Keep it short and descriptive

3–5 words is ideal. Include the main keyword. Google truncates URLs in results after about 70 characters, so shorter slugs are more readable in SERPs.

2

Use hyphens, not underscores

Google treats hyphens as word separators. Underscores join words together — "blue_sky" is read as one word "bluesky" in crawlers.

3

Avoid special characters

Special characters (?, &, %, #) need to be URL-encoded, making URLs ugly and hard to share. Only letters, numbers, and hyphens.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a URL slug? ▼
A URL slug is the human-readable, URL-safe portion of a web address — e.g. in /blog/how-to-make-coffee, the slug is how-to-make-coffee.
Why use hyphens instead of underscores? ▼
Google treats hyphens as word separators in URLs, making them better for SEO. Underscores join words as one term. Use hyphens for public-facing URLs.
What characters are removed from the slug? ▼
Accents are normalised (e-acute becomes e), then all characters that are not letters, numbers, or the chosen separator are removed.
