Will paraphrasing remove plagiarism?
Paraphrasing changes the wording but keeps the same ideas. For academic work, you still need to cite sources even when paraphrasing. It is not a substitute for proper citation. Use it to express ideas in your own words alongside correct attribution.
Does it change the meaning of my text?
No — our AI is instructed to preserve the original meaning exactly. It only changes the words, sentence structure, and phrasing. Always read the result to confirm it says what you intended.
