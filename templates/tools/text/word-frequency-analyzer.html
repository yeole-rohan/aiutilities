Frequently Asked Questions

What are stopwords? ▼
Stopwords are common words like "the", "a", "is", and "and" that are filtered out because they carry no meaningful content. The tool uses a 150+ word English stopword list.
Can I use this to find keywords in my content? ▼
Yes — after filtering stopwords, the most frequent words are your document's main topics. This is useful for SEO keyword density analysis.
Is the analysis case-sensitive? ▼
No — "Apple" and "apple" are counted as the same word. All text is lowercased before analysis.
