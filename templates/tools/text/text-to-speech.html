Frequently Asked Questions

Which browsers support this tool? ▼
Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox all support the Web Speech API. Chrome and Edge have the most voices. Firefox support may vary by OS.
Are the voices stored on my device? ▼
Yes — the voices are provided by your operating system and browser. No audio is sent to any server.
Can I change the language? ▼
Yes — select a voice labelled with a language code like en-GB, fr-FR, or es-ES to hear text spoken in that language.
