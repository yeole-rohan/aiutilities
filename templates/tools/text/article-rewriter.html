Frequently Asked Questions

Will the rewritten article pass plagiarism checkers?
Our AI rewrites content at a deep level — rephrasing sentences, varying structure, and improving flow. The result is substantially different from the source. However, if the original contained common facts or phrases, some similarity may remain. Always review before publishing.
Does it change the facts in my article?
The AI is instructed to preserve all key facts and information from the original. However, AI can occasionally misinterpret complex passages. Always proofread the output, especially for technical, medical, legal, or financial content.
How long can the article be?
Up to 10,000 characters (~1,500 words) per rewrite. For longer articles, split into sections (intro + body + conclusion) and rewrite each separately, then combine them.
Can I use this to rewrite competitor content?
Rewriting published content from other sources and publishing it as your own is copyright infringement, even if the wording is changed. This tool is intended for rewriting your own existing content — such as updating old posts, adapting content for new channels, or refreshing web copy.
