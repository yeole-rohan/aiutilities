Why Count Characters?

Social Media Limits

Twitter/X: 280 chars · LinkedIn: 3,000 · Instagram bio: 150 · Meta ads headline: 40. Stay within limits before posting.

SEO Meta Tags

Meta title: 50–60 chars · Meta description: 150–160 chars. Staying in range ensures Google displays your tags in full.

Content Planning

Check word count for blog posts, essays, and reports. Reading time helps set expectations for readers before they start.

Academic Writing

Essays and assignments often have strict word count limits. Track progress and trim or expand as needed to hit your target.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is reading time calculated? ▼
Reading time is based on the average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute (wpm). The estimate is displayed in minutes and seconds. Actual reading time varies by complexity and reader.
Is my text stored or sent anywhere? ▼
No. All counting happens in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is sent to any server. Your text stays private on your device.
How are sentences counted? ▼
Sentences are counted by splitting on period, exclamation mark, and question mark followed by a space or end of text. Abbreviations (e.g., Mr., Dr.) may slightly over-count sentences.
