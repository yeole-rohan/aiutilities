Why Rewrite Sentences?

🔄

Avoid repetition

If you've used the same sentence structure three times in a paragraph, rewriting one or two breaks the monotony and improves readability.

🎯

Change register

Turn a casual sentence formal (or vice versa) to match your audience — academic papers, client emails, blog posts, and social captions all need different registers.

✂️

Cut word count

A rewrite can often say the same thing in half the words. The "simple and concise" style strips filler and passive constructions.

🌍

Improve non-native English

If English isn't your first language, rewriting a sentence you're not sure about shows you native-sounding alternatives you can model in future writing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between this and the Paraphrasing Tool?
The Paraphrasing Tool rewrites entire paragraphs or passages and focuses on producing one polished version. This Sentence Rewriter works at the individual sentence level and gives you multiple structural variations — useful for comparing options and understanding how sentence structure affects meaning and tone.
Can it rewrite sentences in other languages?
The tool is optimised for English. It can attempt rewrites in other languages (French, Spanish, German, Portuguese) with reasonable results, but quality will vary. For English it performs at a high level across all style options.
