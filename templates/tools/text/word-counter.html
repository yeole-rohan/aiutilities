Frequently Asked Questions

How does the word counter count words? ▼
It splits your text on whitespace (spaces, tabs, newlines) and counts non-empty segments. Hyphenated words like step-by-step are counted as one word.
Does it count characters with or without spaces? ▼
Both — the tool shows characters with spaces and characters without spaces as separate stats.
What is the reading time estimate based on? ▼
It assumes an average reading speed of 200 words per minute, which is typical for an adult reader skimming online content.
Is there a maximum text length? ▼
No server limit — everything runs in your browser, so it handles very large documents as fast as your device allows.
