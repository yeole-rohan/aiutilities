Frequently Asked Questions

What format is the output? ▼
The trimmed audio is saved as an MP3 at 192 kbps — a universally compatible format suitable for sharing, streaming, and most audio uses.
How do I find exact timestamps? ▼
Use the audio player's progress bar and timer to scrub to the moment you want. The current time in seconds is displayed below the player — note it down and enter it in the start/end fields.
Is my audio stored anywhere? ▼
No. Your file is read directly from your device into browser memory. No data is sent to any server. Closing the tab discards everything.
