Bitrate Guide

64 kbps

Very small files. Acceptable for voice recordings, audiobooks, and speech where music quality is not needed. Smallest possible output.

96 kbps

Good podcast quality. Suitable for spoken-word content, interviews, and lectures. A step up from 64 kbps with noticeably better clarity.

128 kbps

The standard for music streaming. Transparent to most ears for pop, rock, and electronic music. Recommended default for compression.

192 kbps

High quality — used by Spotify's standard tier. Excellent for most music genres. Good balance between file size and fidelity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much smaller will the file be? ▼
The size reduction depends on the original bitrate. A 320 kbps MP3 compressed to 128 kbps will be roughly 60% smaller. WAV files converted to 128 kbps MP3 can shrink by 90%+.
Can I compress an already-compressed MP3? ▼
Yes, but re-encoding a lossy file increases quality loss. If the source is already at 128 kbps, compressing to 64 kbps will produce audible artifacts. Start from the highest-quality source available.
What is the output format? ▼
The output is always an MP3 file — the most universally compatible audio format for playback on phones, computers, and streaming platforms.
