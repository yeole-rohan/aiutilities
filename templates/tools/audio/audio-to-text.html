Frequently Asked Questions

Which browsers support this? ▼
The Web Speech API is best supported in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge. Firefox and Safari have partial support. For the best experience, use Chrome on desktop.
Is my audio sent to a server? ▼
The Web Speech API may send audio to the browser vendor's servers (Google/Microsoft) for recognition — this is handled by the browser, not by us. We never receive your audio or transcript.
Can I transcribe an existing audio file? ▼
This tool transcribes live microphone input. To transcribe an audio file, play it through your speakers while recording, or use a dedicated file-upload transcription service.
