Frequently Asked Questions

Does converting FLAC to MP3 lose quality? ▼
Yes — MP3 is lossy. But at 192 kbps or higher, the difference is imperceptible to most listeners. For archival purposes, keep the original FLAC file.
What bitrate should I choose? ▼
192 kbps is the sweet spot for streaming and sharing. Use 320 kbps for music production or critical listening. 128 kbps is fine for voice-only content like podcasts.
Is there a file size limit? ▼
No hard limit, but files over 500 MB may be slow or cause memory issues in the browser. For very large files, a desktop app like Audacity or native FFmpeg is faster.
