Frequently Asked Questions

What barcode format should I use for retail? ▼
EAN-13 is the standard for retail products globally. UPC-A is used in North America. Both require a registered GS1 prefix for commercial use. CODE128 is best for internal tracking where no registry is required.
What is the difference between SVG and PNG? ▼
SVG is a vector format that scales to any size without pixelation — ideal for printing labels. PNG is a raster image; for print use, make the bar width larger before downloading to get enough resolution.
Why does EAN-13 say "invalid" for some numbers? ▼
EAN-13 requires exactly 12 digits (the 13th is a check digit added automatically). Enter only the first 12 digits — the library computes the correct check digit for you.
