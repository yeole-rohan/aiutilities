Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't my QR code be read? ▼
Common causes: the image is too blurry, low contrast, too small, or the QR code is partially obscured. Try a higher resolution screenshot or photograph in better lighting.
Is my QR code data private? ▼
Yes. All decoding uses the jsQR library running entirely in your browser. No image data is sent to any server.
What types of QR codes can this read? ▼
Standard QR codes (ISO/IEC 18004) including all versions (1–40) and error correction levels. This includes URLs, plain text, WiFi credentials, contact cards (vCard), email, and phone number QR codes.
