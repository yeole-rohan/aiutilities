Frequently Asked Questions

What barcode formats are supported? ▼
EAN-8, EAN-13, UPC-A, UPC-E, CODE39, CODE93, CODE128, Codabar, ITF, DataMatrix, PDF417, Aztec, and QR codes — powered by the ZXing multi-format decoding library.
Why is my barcode not being detected? ▼
The most common causes are blur, low contrast, or the barcode being too small in the image. Take a photo with good lighting and ensure the barcode fills at least 30% of the image frame.
Is this barcode reader private? ▼
Yes. All decoding runs in your browser using the ZXing library. No images or barcode data are sent to any server.
