Frequently Asked Questions

What error correction level should I choose? ▼
Use Medium (M) for most digital uses. Use High (H) if the QR code will be printed and may get dirty or partially damaged — it adds redundancy so the code can still be read even if up to 30% is obscured.
What is the minimum print size for a QR code? ▼
The minimum recommended print size is 2 cm × 2 cm (about 0.8 inches) for a standard smartphone camera at a scanning distance of 20 cm. Larger QR codes with more data need to be printed bigger to remain scannable.
Do QR codes expire? ▼
QR codes generated here embed the content directly — they never expire. Dynamic QR codes (from paid services) redirect through a short URL that can be changed; those expire if the service shuts down. Static QR codes (like these) work forever.
