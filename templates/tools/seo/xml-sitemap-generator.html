Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I upload my sitemap? ▼
Upload sitemap.xml to the root of your domain so it is accessible at https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml. Then submit the URL in Google Search Console under Sitemaps and Bing Webmaster Tools.
What does priority mean in a sitemap? ▼
Priority (0.0–1.0) is a hint to crawlers about the relative importance of pages within your site. It does not affect rankings directly. Most pages should be 0.5; your homepage and key landing pages can be 1.0.
How many URLs can a sitemap have? ▼
A single sitemap file can contain up to 50,000 URLs and must be no larger than 50MB uncompressed. For larger sites, use a sitemap index file that points to multiple individual sitemaps.
