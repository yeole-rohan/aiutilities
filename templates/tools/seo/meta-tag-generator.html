Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ideal meta title length? ▼
Google typically displays 50–60 characters of a title tag. Titles longer than ~60 characters may be truncated in search results. Include your primary keyword near the beginning.
What is Open Graph? ▼
Open Graph (OG) tags control how your page appears when shared on Facebook, LinkedIn, Slack, and other platforms. The og:title, og:description, and og:image tags define the title, text, and preview image shown in link previews.
What size should my OG image be? ▼
The recommended OG image size is 1200×630 pixels (1.91:1 ratio). Use JPEG or PNG format. Minimum size is 600×315px but larger images look sharper on high-DPI displays.
What does noindex mean? ▼
noindex tells search engines not to include this page in their index. Use it for admin pages, duplicate content, or pages under construction. nofollow tells crawlers not to follow links on the page.
