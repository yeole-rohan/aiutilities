Frequently Asked Questions

Where should I put robots.txt? ▼
Robots.txt must be placed in the root of your domain at exactly https://yourdomain.com/robots.txt. It does not work in subdirectories.
Does robots.txt prevent pages from being indexed? ▼
Not directly. Disallowing a URL only tells bots not to crawl it; they may still index it if they find links pointing to it. To prevent indexing, use a noindex meta tag on the page itself.
Should I block AI bots? ▼
Whether to block AI training bots is a personal or business decision. If you do not want your content used to train AI models, blocking GPTBot, CCBot, and similar bots is a reasonable step, though compliance is voluntary.
