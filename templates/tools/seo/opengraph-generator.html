Frequently Asked Questions

Which platforms use Open Graph tags? ▼
Facebook, LinkedIn, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp, and most modern platforms read OG tags. Twitter/X uses its own Twitter Card tags (also generated here), though it falls back to OG tags if Twitter Card tags are absent.
Why is my OG image not showing? ▼
Platforms cache OG data. After adding tags, use the Facebook Sharing Debugger or LinkedIn Post Inspector to force a re-scrape. Also ensure your image is publicly accessible (not behind a login) and at least 200×200px.
What is the best OG image size? ▼
Use 1200×630px (1.91:1 ratio) for best results across all platforms. Minimum is 600×315px. Keep important content away from the edges as some platforms crop images.
