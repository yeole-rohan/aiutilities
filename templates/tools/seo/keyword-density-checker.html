Why Check Keyword Density?

On-Page SEO

Check if your target keyword appears at an appropriate density (1–3% is typical). Too low and the page may not rank — too high risks keyword stuffing penalties.

Content Optimization

Identify which topics dominate your content and where to add related keywords, synonyms, or LSI terms to improve topical depth.

Competitor Analysis

Paste a competitor's content to see which keywords they emphasize — useful for understanding their on-page strategy and finding gaps.

Content Auditing

Quickly assess old blog posts or landing pages to see if they're optimized for the right keywords before a site migration or refresh.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ideal keyword density for SEO? ▼
There is no magic number, but most SEO experts suggest 1–3% for your primary keyword. Focus on natural writing first — Google prioritizes context and topical relevance over exact keyword repetition.
What are stop words? ▼
Stop words are common words like "the", "and", "is", "in", "a". They appear in almost every text and don't carry SEO value. Filtering them shows only meaningful keywords.
Does it support multi-word keyword phrases? ▼
Currently the tool analyzes single words (unigrams). For specific keyword phrase tracking (e.g., "keyword density checker"), search the page text manually using Ctrl+F or a dedicated rank tracker.
