Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Google sometimes show different text than my meta description? ▼
Google may rewrite your snippet if it thinks a different portion of your page better matches the search query. Writing relevant, complete descriptions reduces the chance of Google overriding them.
How long should my meta description be? ▼
Google truncates descriptions at approximately 160 characters on desktop and 120 on mobile. Aim for 150–160 characters to get the most text shown without being cut off.
Does the meta description affect SEO rankings? ▼
The meta description is not a direct ranking factor, but it strongly affects click-through rate (CTR). A compelling description that matches user intent can significantly increase organic traffic.
