Frequently Asked Questions

What is a standard tip percentage? ▼
15–20% is standard in the US for sit-down restaurants. 18–20% is the current norm. 25%+ for exceptional service. 10% for counter service or buffets.
Should I tip before or after tax? ▼
Etiquette varies — most diners tip on the pre-tax subtotal, but tipping on the full amount including tax is also common.
How do I split unequally? ▼
The calculator splits evenly. For custom splits, adjust each person's share manually after calculating the total tip.
