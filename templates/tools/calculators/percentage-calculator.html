Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage increase? ▼
Use mode 2 (Percentage Change): enter the original value as A and the new value as B. A positive result is an increase; negative means a decrease.
What is X% of Y? ▼
This is the most common calculation — e.g. "What is 15% of 200?" equals 30. Use mode 1 for this.
How do I find what percentage X is of Y? ▼
Use mode 3: the result is (X / Y) × 100. For example, 30 is 15% of 200.
