Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI range? ▼
The WHO classifies BMI 18.5–24.9 as "Normal weight". Below 18.5 is Underweight, 25–29.9 is Overweight, and 30+ is Obese.
Is BMI accurate for athletes? ▼
BMI does not distinguish between muscle and fat, so muscular people may have a high BMI without excess body fat. It is a screening tool, not a clinical diagnosis.
What formula does the calculator use? ▼
BMI = weight (kg) divided by height squared (m squared). For imperial inputs, weight and height are first converted to metric.
