Frequently Asked Questions

What is a standard drink? ▼
In the US, one standard drink contains 14 grams of pure alcohol — equivalent to 12 oz regular beer (5%), 5 oz wine (12%), or 1.5 oz spirits (40%).
What formula is used? ▼
The Widmark formula: BAC = (alcohol in grams / (body weight in grams × r)) × 100 − 0.015 × hours. r = 0.73 for males, 0.66 for females.
What is the legal BAC limit for driving? ▼
In the US and most of Europe the limit is 0.08% BAC. Some countries use 0.05%. Commercial drivers and under-21s face stricter limits.
