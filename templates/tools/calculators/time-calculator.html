Frequently Asked Questions

Can I enter more than 59 minutes or seconds? ▼
Yes — you can enter any number. 90 minutes is automatically converted to 1 hour 30 minutes. The calculator handles overflow and underflow automatically.
What happens if the result is negative? ▼
If subtractions exceed additions, the result is shown as a negative duration (e.g., -0:15:00). This can be useful for checking how much time is over or under a target.
What does "decimal hours" mean? ▼
Decimal hours express the duration as a single number — e.g., 1 hour 30 minutes = 1.5 hours. This is useful for billing (e.g., at $50/hr, 1.5h = $75).
