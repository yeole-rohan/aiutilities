Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between DEG and RAD? ▼
DEG (degrees) and RAD (radians) are two ways to measure angles. Most everyday users should use DEG. RAD is standard in mathematics and physics.
How do I use the ANS button? ▼
ANS stores the last calculated result. After pressing =, type a new operation and the previous answer is used automatically.
What are the keyboard shortcuts? ▼
Press Enter to evaluate an expression, Backspace to delete the last character, and Escape to clear.
