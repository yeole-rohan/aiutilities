Frequently Asked Questions

What is an amortisation schedule? ▼
An amortisation schedule shows each monthly payment broken down into principal (reducing the balance) and interest. Early payments are mostly interest; later ones are mostly principal.
What formula is used for monthly payment? ▼
PMT = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is principal, r is monthly rate, and n is number of payments.
Can I use this for car loans? ▼
Yes — enter the car price minus any down payment as the principal, your loan APR as the rate, and your term in months.
