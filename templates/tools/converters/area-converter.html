Common Area Comparisons

1 Hectare

= 10,000 m² = 2.471 acres = 107,639 ft². About the size of a football pitch plus end zones. Used in agriculture and land measurement.

1 Acre

= 4,047 m² = 43,560 ft² = 0.4047 hectares. Roughly the size of a US football field (without end zones). Standard unit in US and UK land sales.

1 km²

= 1,000,000 m² = 100 hectares = 247.1 acres = 0.386 square miles. Used for city districts and national park measurements.

1 Square Mile

= 2.59 km² = 259 hectares = 640 acres = 27,878,400 ft². Standard US land survey unit — townships are 36 square miles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between hectares and acres? ▼
A hectare (ha) is a metric unit equal to 10,000 m². An acre is an imperial unit equal to 4,047 m². One hectare equals about 2.471 acres. Hectares are used in most countries; acres in the US and UK.
How precise are the conversions? ▼
Conversions use internationally standardized exact values where available. Results are displayed to 6 significant figures — sufficient for surveying, construction, and land transactions.
What unit does real estate use? ▼
It depends on the country. The US uses square feet (for buildings) and acres (for land). The UK uses square feet for buildings and acres or hectares for land. Most other countries use m² and hectares.
