Frequently Asked Questions

Where do the exchange rates come from? ▼
Rates are fetched from Open Exchange Rates (open.er-api.com), a free public API that aggregates mid-market rates from financial data providers. Rates are updated daily.
Are these rates suitable for financial transactions? ▼
These are mid-market rates for reference only. Banks and money transfer services add a margin (typically 0.5%–3%), so the actual rate you receive will differ. Always confirm with your financial institution.
How often are rates updated? ▼
The free API tier updates rates once per day. For real-time trading rates, consult a live financial platform or your broker.
