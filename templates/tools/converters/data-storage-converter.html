Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between GB and GiB? ▼
1 GB (gigabyte) = 1,000,000,000 bytes (decimal). 1 GiB (gibibyte) = 1,073,741,824 bytes (binary). Hard drive manufacturers use decimal GB, while operating systems have traditionally used binary GiB.
Why does my 1 TB hard drive show less space in Windows? ▼
Windows historically reports storage in GiB but labels them GB. A 1 TB (1,000 GB) drive shows roughly 931 GiB in Windows.
How many bytes in a terabyte? ▼
1 TB = 1,000,000,000,000 bytes (10 to the 12th) in decimal. 1 TiB = 1,099,511,627,776 bytes (2 to the 40th) in binary.
