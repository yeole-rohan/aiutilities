Frequently Asked Questions

What is UTC? ▼
UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the primary time standard used worldwide. All other time zones are expressed as offsets from UTC — for example, New York is UTC-5 in winter and UTC-4 during daylight saving time.
How does Daylight Saving Time affect conversions? ▼
This tool uses the IANA time zone database via JavaScript's Intl API, which automatically accounts for Daylight Saving Time (DST) transitions. The converted time will always reflect the correct DST-adjusted offset for the selected date.
What is the International Date Line? ▼
The International Date Line (IDL) runs roughly along 180° longitude in the Pacific Ocean. Crossing it from west to east moves you back one calendar day; crossing eastward moves you forward. This is why some time zones are UTC+13 or UTC+14 while others near them are UTC-11.
