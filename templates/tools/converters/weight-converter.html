Frequently Asked Questions

How many pounds in a kilogram? ▼
1 kg = 2.20462 lbs. Conversely, 1 lb is approximately 0.453592 kg.
What is a stone in pounds? ▼
1 stone = 14 pounds. Stone is used informally for body weight in the UK and Ireland.
How many grams in an ounce? ▼
1 avoirdupois ounce = 28.3495 grams. Note this is the everyday ounce, not the troy ounce used for precious metals.
