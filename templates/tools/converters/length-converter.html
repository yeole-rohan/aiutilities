Frequently Asked Questions

How many centimetres in an inch? ▼
Exactly 2.54 cm per inch. So 1 foot = 30.48 cm, and 1 metre = 39.37 inches.
How many kilometres in a mile? ▼
1 mile = 1.60934 km. Conversely, 1 km is approximately 0.621 miles.
What is a nautical mile? ▼
A nautical mile (1852 m) is used in aviation and maritime navigation. It is based on one minute of arc of latitude along the Earth's surface.
