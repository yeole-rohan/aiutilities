Frequently Asked Questions

How many km/h in 1 mph? ▼
1 mph = 1.60934 km/h. Conversely, 1 km/h is approximately 0.621 mph.
What is a knot? ▼
A knot is 1 nautical mile per hour (1.852 km/h). It is used in maritime and aviation navigation.
What is Mach 1? ▼
Mach 1 is the speed of sound — approximately 343 m/s (1235 km/h) at sea level and 20°C. The exact value varies with temperature and altitude.
