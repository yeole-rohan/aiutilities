Frequently Asked Questions

What is the formula to convert Celsius to Fahrenheit? ▼
F = (C × 9/5) + 32. For example, 100°C = 212°F, which is the boiling point of water at sea level.
What is absolute zero? ▼
Absolute zero — the coldest possible temperature — is −273.15°C, 0 Kelvin, or −459.67°F.
What is normal body temperature? ▼
Normal body temperature is 37°C (98.6°F). A fever is generally considered above 38°C (100.4°F).
