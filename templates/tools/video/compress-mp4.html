Frequently Asked Questions

Is my video really not uploaded anywhere? ▼
Correct. FFmpeg runs as WebAssembly entirely inside your browser tab. Your video is read from disk into browser memory and never sent to any server.
How much will it compress? ▼
Results depend on the source video and quality setting. Typical savings range from 30–70%. Videos already well-compressed (e.g. from YouTube) may see less reduction.
Why does it take a while? ▼
Video encoding is CPU-intensive. FFmpeg WASM runs on your processor inside the browser — slower than a native app, but completely private. Large files may take a few minutes.
