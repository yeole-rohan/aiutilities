Frequently Asked Questions

Can I trim without re-encoding (lossless)? ▼
This tool re-encodes using H.264 for maximum compatibility. For lossless stream-copy trimming, use a native FFmpeg command with -c copy on your desktop.
What time format should I use? ▼
Use HH:MM:SS format — e.g., 00:01:30 for 1 minute 30 seconds. The preview player shows the current time in the same format.
Can I make multiple cuts? ▼
This tool extracts a single continuous segment. To make multiple cuts, trim each segment separately and combine the results with the Merge PDF… or a video editor.
