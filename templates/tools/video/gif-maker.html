Frequently Asked Questions

How many frames can I use? ▼
There's no hard limit, but large numbers of high-resolution frames may be slow. For best performance, keep frames under 20 and use a reasonable max-width like 480–720px.
Why does the GIF look lower quality than the originals? ▼
GIF format is limited to 256 colors per frame, which causes dithering artifacts — especially on photos. For high-quality animations, consider WebP animated images or MP4 video instead.
Are my images private? ▼
Yes — all GIF creation runs in your browser using gif.js. Images are never uploaded to any server.
