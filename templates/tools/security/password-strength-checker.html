Frequently Asked Questions

What is password entropy? ▼
Entropy measures how unpredictable a password is, in bits. Higher entropy means harder to crack. A 12-character password using all 4 character types has roughly 78 bits of entropy.
What is the crack time estimate? ▼
It estimates how long a brute-force attack at 10 billion guesses per second would take. This is approximately the speed of a modern GPU attack.
Is my password sent anywhere? ▼
No — the strength check runs entirely in JavaScript in your browser. Your password is never transmitted to a server.
What makes a strong password? ▼
Length is the most important factor. At least 12 characters, using uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Avoid dictionary words, names, and dates.
