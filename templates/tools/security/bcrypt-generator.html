Frequently Asked Questions

What is bcrypt? ▼
Bcrypt is a password hashing algorithm designed by Niels Provos and David Mazières in 1999. It incorporates a salt to protect against rainbow table attacks and an adaptive cost factor that can be increased over time to remain resistant to brute-force attacks as hardware improves.
Why does the same password produce different hashes? ▼
Bcrypt automatically generates a random salt for each hash. This means the same password hashed twice will produce two different hash strings — but both will verify correctly against the original password. The salt is stored inside the hash string.
What cost factor should I use in production? ▼
The OWASP recommendation is cost factor 10 as a minimum. Each increment doubles the computation time. Choose the highest factor your server can handle in under 100ms per login — typically 10–12 for most applications.
