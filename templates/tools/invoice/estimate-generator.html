Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between an estimate and a quote? ▼
An estimate is an approximate price that may change. A quote is a fixed price commitment. Estimates are common in construction and services where exact costs are hard to predict upfront. Use estimates for complex jobs and formal quotes for simpler, well-defined projects.
Should I include tax in the estimate? ▼
Best practice is to show the pre-tax subtotal, tax amount separately, and the total including tax. This helps clients understand the full cost and simplifies your record-keeping.
How long should an estimate be valid? ▼
Typically 30 days is standard, though it depends on your industry and how quickly material costs change. Construction estimates may be valid for 7–14 days due to volatile material prices. Set the "Valid Until" date accordingly.
