Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a receipt and an invoice? ▼
An invoice is a request for payment sent before payment is made. A receipt is proof of payment issued after payment has been received. Receipts confirm the transaction is complete.
Is this receipt legally valid? ▼
A receipt's legal standing depends on local regulations. For most informal transactions, this receipt is sufficient. For tax purposes or formal business use, consult an accountant about your jurisdiction's requirements.
Is my data stored? ▼
No. All data is held in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server. Print or save the PDF before leaving the page.
