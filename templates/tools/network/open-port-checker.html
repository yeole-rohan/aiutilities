Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't browsers scan all ports? ▼
For security, browsers block raw TCP socket connections from JavaScript. Websites could otherwise use your browser to port-scan machines on your local network (a form of CSRF). Use nmap, Netcat (nc), or Nessus for full port scanning.
What port does SSH use? ▼
SSH uses TCP port 22 by default. Many administrators change this to a non-standard port (e.g. 2222) to reduce automated brute-force attempts — though this is security through obscurity, not a real defense.
What is a "well-known port"? ▼
Well-known ports (0–1023) are assigned by IANA to standardized services. Ports 1024–49151 are registered ports for applications. Ports 49152–65535 are ephemeral (dynamic) ports used for temporary client connections.
