Frequently Asked Questions

What is CIDR notation? ▼
CIDR (Classless Inter-Domain Routing) notation combines an IP address with a prefix length — e.g., 192.168.1.0/24. The /24 means the first 24 bits are the network portion, leaving 8 bits for hosts (256 addresses, 254 usable).
Why are 2 addresses unusable? ▼
The first address is the network address and the last is the broadcast address — both are reserved and cannot be assigned to hosts. A /24 has 256 addresses with 254 usable.
What is a wildcard mask? ▼
A wildcard mask is the inverse of a subnet mask — 0 bits match, 1 bits are "don't care." For 255.255.255.0 the wildcard is 0.0.0.255. Wildcard masks are used in Cisco ACLs and OSPF configurations.
