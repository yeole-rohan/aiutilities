Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between margin and markup? ▼
Margin is profit as a percentage of revenue: (Revenue − Cost) / Revenue. Markup is profit as a percentage of cost: (Revenue − Cost) / Cost. A 50% markup equals a 33% margin. They measure the same profit but against different bases.
What is a good profit margin? ▼
It depends heavily on the industry. Software and SaaS companies often exceed 70%. Retail typically runs 20–50% gross margin. Restaurants can be as low as 3–9% net margin. Compare your margins to industry benchmarks for meaningful context.
How do I improve my profit margin? ▼
You can improve margin by increasing prices, reducing COGS through better supplier negotiation, cutting operating expenses, upselling higher-margin products, or improving operational efficiency to serve more customers at the same cost.
