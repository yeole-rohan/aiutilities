Etsy Listing SEO Tips

🏷️

Title: Front-load Keywords

Etsy's algorithm weighs the first few words most. Put your primary keyword (what buyers actually search) at the start, followed by descriptors like material, occasion, and style.

🏷️

Use All 13 Tags

Etsy gives you 13 tags — use every one. Mix broad terms (handmade gift), mid-tail (personalized cutting board), and long-tail (custom oak cutting board wedding gift). No repeats from title needed.

📖

Description: Story First

Only the first few lines show before "Read more." Hook buyers with the feeling or occasion — who it's for, what makes it special — before listing specs.

📷

Photos Drive Conversions

Use all 10 photo slots. Include lifestyle shots (product in use), scale reference, close-up of detail, and packaging. The first photo is your thumbnail — make it bright and clear.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should an Etsy title be?
Etsy allows up to 140 characters. Aim for 120–130 to ensure it's fully visible in search results. Front-load with your primary keyword, then add secondary keywords separated by commas or pipes.
Should I repeat keywords from my title in my tags?
No. Etsy's algorithm already combines your title and tag signals — repeating the same phrase wastes a tag slot. Use tags to cover different buyer intents, synonyms, and related searches you couldn't fit in the title.
What makes an Etsy description convert well?
Open with the emotional hook (who it's for, the occasion). Follow with key details: size, material, what's included. Then add production and shipping info. Close with a call to action (message me, check my shop). Use line breaks for readability.
