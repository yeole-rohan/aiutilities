What Makes a Review Trustworthy?

1

Specificity beats vagueness

"Battery lasts 18 hours on my commute" is more credible than "great battery." Specific details signal a real user.

2

Include at least one negative

All-positive reviews look fake. A minor con ("the app could be better") actually increases trust by showing balanced judgment.

3

Mention your use context

Readers want to know if your situation matches theirs. "As a daily gym-goer" or "for light home cooking" helps them calibrate relevance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use this to write reviews for Amazon, Google, or Trustpilot?
Yes — this tool generates review text you can use on any platform. Always edit the output to match your actual experience before posting. Platform terms require reviews to reflect genuine use.
How long should a product review be?
150–300 words is the sweet spot for most platforms. Long enough to be useful, short enough to be read. Amazon Vine reviewers average around 250 words. For Google or Trustpilot, 100–150 words is sufficient.
