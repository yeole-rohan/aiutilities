Frequently Asked Questions

What is dimensional weight? ▼
Dimensional (DIM) weight is calculated as (L × W × H) / 139 for UPS and FedEx. If the DIM weight is greater than the actual weight, carriers charge based on DIM weight. This penalises light but bulky packages.
What is a shipping zone? ▼
Zones (1–8) represent the distance between origin and destination. Zone 1 is the closest (same region) and Zone 8 is the farthest. Carriers use zip code pairs to determine the exact zone — rates increase with zone number.
