US States With No Sales Tax

Oregon

No statewide sales tax. Relies heavily on income tax revenue. No local sales taxes either — 0% total.

Montana

No state or local sales tax. Some resort areas may have local option taxes, but general retail purchases are tax-free.

New Hampshire

No sales tax. Live Free or Die state funds government primarily through property taxes and business taxes.

Delaware & Alaska

No statewide sales tax. Delaware has no local taxes either. Alaska has no state tax but some local sales taxes apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are state rates the only rate I need? ▼
No — most US states allow counties and cities to add local sales taxes on top of the state rate. For example, California's base rate is 7.25% but Los Angeles County adds more, making the combined rate 10.25%. Use the custom rate field to enter the combined rate for your location.
How is sales tax calculated? ▼
Tax amount = Price × Rate ÷ 100. Total = Price + Tax amount. For example: $100 × 8.5% = $8.50 tax → $108.50 total.
Are the state rates current? ▼
State rates are based on 2024 statewide base rates. These change occasionally — always verify the current rate with your state's department of revenue for official tax filings.
