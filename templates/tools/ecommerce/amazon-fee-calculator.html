Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Amazon referral fee? ▼
The referral fee is a percentage of the sale price that Amazon charges every seller. It varies by category — 15% is the most common rate, but it ranges from 6% (some electronics) to 20% (fine jewelry). There is typically a minimum fee of $0.30.
Is FBA or FBM better? ▼
FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) gives you Prime eligibility and hands off logistics but adds fulfillment and storage fees. FBM (Fulfilled by Merchant) gives you more control and avoids FBA fees but requires your own shipping and loses Prime badge. The best choice depends on your margins, volume, and product type.
