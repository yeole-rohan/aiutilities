Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a cookie policy?
Yes, if your website uses any cookies beyond strictly necessary ones (analytics, ads, social media embeds), GDPR and ePrivacy Directive require you to inform users and obtain consent. Most websites need a cookie policy and a consent banner.
What's the difference between a cookie policy and a privacy policy?
A privacy policy covers all personal data collection and processing. A cookie policy specifically explains what cookies your site uses, their purpose, and how users can control them. Some sites combine them; others keep them separate. Both are often required.
Do I need a cookie consent banner too?
Yes, under GDPR, you must obtain informed consent before setting non-essential cookies (analytics, marketing). A cookie policy alone isn't enough — you need a consent mechanism. Essential cookies don't require consent.
What are "essential" cookies?
Essential cookies are necessary for the website to function — session cookies, CSRF tokens, shopping cart cookies, login state. They cannot be disabled without breaking the site and don't require consent under GDPR. Google Analytics and advertising cookies are NOT essential.
