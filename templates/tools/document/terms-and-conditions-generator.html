Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need Terms and Conditions?
Unlike a privacy policy, T&Cs are not legally required in most jurisdictions. However, they're strongly recommended because they: protect your IP, limit your liability, set usage rules, define dispute resolution, and are required by payment processors like Stripe and PayPal.
What's the difference between T&Cs and a Privacy Policy?
A Privacy Policy explains how you handle personal data (required by law in most countries). Terms & Conditions govern how users may use your service, your liability, and legal relationship with users (optional but recommended). Most websites need both.
How do I make users agree to the T&Cs?
Link to them during account registration with a checkbox ("I agree to the Terms & Conditions"). Also link in the footer of every page. For apps, show them on first launch. This creates a "clickwrap" agreement which is enforceable in most courts.
