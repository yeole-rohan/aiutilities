Frequently Asked Questions

When do I need an NDA?
Use an NDA when: sharing a business idea with a potential investor or partner, hiring contractors or freelancers who will see confidential information, discussing a merger or acquisition, or sharing trade secrets, source code, or proprietary processes.
One-way vs mutual — which do I choose?
One-way: you share confidential info with someone (e.g., freelancer, investor pitch). Mutual: both sides share confidential info (e.g., joint venture, M&A discussions). One-way is more common for early-stage conversations.
Is an NDA enforceable?
Yes, when properly drafted, signed, and specific about what constitutes confidential information. Courts enforce NDAs but may not uphold overly broad or unreasonable ones. For high-value situations, have a lawyer review the agreement.
Should I sign an NDA before discussing my startup idea?
Most VCs and accelerators won't sign NDAs for early conversations — it's impractical at their scale. NDAs are more useful when sharing detailed technical information, source code, or financial data with potential partners, employees, or contractors.
