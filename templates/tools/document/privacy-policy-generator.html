Frequently Asked Questions

Do I legally need a privacy policy?
Yes, in most cases. If you collect any personal data (email addresses, analytics, cookies), GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), and many other regulations require a privacy policy. App stores (Google Play, Apple App Store) also require one.
Is this legally binding?
A privacy policy is a legal document when posted on your website and users are informed of it. This template is a starting point — review it to ensure it accurately describes your data practices. For compliance-critical businesses, have a lawyer review it.
How do I add it to my website?
Copy the generated policy and paste it into a dedicated /privacy-policy page on your site. Link to it from your footer, sign-up forms, and cookie consent banner. Update it whenever your data practices change.
How often should I update my privacy policy?
Update it whenever you change what data you collect, how you use it, which third parties you share it with, or when privacy laws change. Add a "Last updated" date to the top of your policy.
