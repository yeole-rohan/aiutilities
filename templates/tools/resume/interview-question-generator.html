Frequently Asked Questions

Can candidates use this tool too?
Absolutely. Candidates can generate likely interview questions for a role they're applying to and prepare STAR-method answers in advance. This is one of the most effective interview prep strategies — practice answering the generated questions out loud before your interview.
What is the STAR method?
STAR stands for Situation, Task, Action, Result. It's the gold standard format for answering behavioral questions: describe the context (Situation), your role (Task), what you did (Action), and what happened (Result, ideally with a number). Keeps answers concise and impactful.
How many interview questions should I prepare for an interview panel?
For a 45–60 minute interview, plan 8–12 questions plus 2–3 follow-up probes per question. Not all questions will be asked — have extras ready. For panel interviews, each interviewer typically covers a different area (technical, behavioral, culture) so coordinate question types in advance.
