Frequently Asked Questions

How do I copy text from my PDF resume?
Open your PDF in Chrome, Adobe Reader, or any PDF viewer. Press Ctrl+A (or Cmd+A on Mac) to select all text, then Ctrl+C to copy. Paste it into the text area above. If the text is garbled, your PDF is a scanned image — use our OCR PDF tool first.
What ATS score is considered good?
Aim for 75+/100 as a general benchmark. Most ATS systems pass resumes that score above their threshold (usually 60–70% keyword match). Scoring 85+ means your resume is very well optimized for ATS parsing.
Should I include the job description?
Yes — strongly recommended. When you include the job description, the AI gives you specific missing keywords from that JD, making the recommendations directly actionable for that application. Without it, you get general ATS advice.
Is my resume data private?
Yes. Your resume text is sent to the AI for analysis and is not stored in our database. We do not retain your resume content after generating results. Remove personal details like phone numbers before pasting if you prefer extra privacy.
