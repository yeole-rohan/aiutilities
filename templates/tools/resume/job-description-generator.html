Frequently Asked Questions

Why is inclusive language in JDs important?
Studies show that masculine-coded words in job descriptions (e.g., "dominant," "competitive," "ninja") deter women from applying, even when equally qualified. Inclusive language broadens your candidate pool and improves team diversity.
Should I list a degree requirement?
Only if the job genuinely requires a specific degree (e.g., medical, legal, engineering). For most tech and business roles, listing "degree required" excludes talented candidates with equivalent experience. Our AI avoids unnecessary degree requirements by default.
Can I use this for any industry?
Yes — tech, marketing, finance, healthcare, education, retail, hospitality, manufacturing, and more. The more details you provide about responsibilities and skills, the more industry-specific the output.
