Frequently Asked Questions

Should I use the cover letter as-is?
Use it as a strong first draft. Add specific numbers and achievements (e.g., "grew sales by 40%"), tweak the tone to match your personality, and verify all company-specific details. A personalized letter always outperforms a generic one.
How long is the generated cover letter?
3 concise paragraphs — roughly 250–350 words. This is the optimal length for cover letters. Long enough to demonstrate fit, short enough for hiring managers who skim.
Does it work for any industry?
Yes — tech, marketing, finance, healthcare, teaching, engineering, and more. The more context you provide about your skills and why you want the role, the more relevant and industry-specific the output will be.
Can I generate multiple versions?
Yes, completely free. Submit the form multiple times with different details or slight variations. Each generation produces a unique letter.
