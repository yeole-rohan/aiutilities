Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a resume summary and objective?
A resume summary focuses on what you offer the employer — your experience, skills, and achievements. A resume objective focuses on what you want from the job. Summaries are preferred for most professionals. Objectives are only appropriate for career changers or recent graduates with little experience.
Should I include a summary on every resume?
Yes, for almost everyone. A well-written summary gives recruiters an instant snapshot of your value before they read further. It's especially important on one-page resumes where space is tight, and for ATS optimization since keywords in the summary are weighted highly.
Should I customize my summary for each application?
Yes — tailor the last sentence or two for each role. Mirror the exact language from the job posting (e.g., if they say "cross-functional collaboration," use that phrase). A targeted summary dramatically improves your ATS score and grabs the recruiter's attention.
