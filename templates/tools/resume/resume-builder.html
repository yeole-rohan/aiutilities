Frequently Asked Questions

Is my resume data saved? ▼
No. All data is held in your browser only and is lost when you close or refresh the page. Always print or save as PDF before leaving. Nothing is sent to any server.
How do I save the resume as a PDF? ▼
Click "Print / Save PDF", then in the print dialog change the Destination to "Save as PDF". In Chrome/Edge you can also set the margins to None for maximum content width.
What format is best for submitting resumes? ▼
PDF is the universally accepted format for resumes. It preserves your formatting exactly across all devices and operating systems. Always submit PDF unless the job posting specifically requests a Word document.
