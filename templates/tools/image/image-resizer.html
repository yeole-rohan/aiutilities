Frequently Asked Questions

Can I resize an image without losing quality? ▼
Enlarging an image always involves some quality loss since pixels must be interpolated. Shrinking an image generally looks good. Use PNG format to avoid compression artefacts and set quality to 100% for the best result.
Is my image uploaded to a server? ▼
No. All processing uses the HTML5 Canvas API inside your browser. Your image data never leaves your device.
What is the maximum image size I can resize? ▼
The limit depends on your browser and device memory. Most modern browsers handle images up to 30–50 MP without issues. Very large images may be slow on mobile devices.
