Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the colour slightly different from what I see? ▼
Screens can display colors differently based on your monitor's color profile and gamma settings. The tool reads raw pixel values from the image data, which may differ from how it appears visually on your calibrated display.
What file types are supported? ▼
Any image format your browser can display works — JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, SVG, AVIF, and more. The image is drawn to a canvas element and pixel data is read using the HTML5 Canvas API.
How is HSL different from RGB? ▼
RGB (Red, Green, Blue) describes a colour by its light components. HSL (Hue, Saturation, Lightness) describes it more intuitively — hue is the colour angle (0–360°), saturation is the intensity, and lightness is how bright or dark it is. HSL is easier for designers to reason about.
