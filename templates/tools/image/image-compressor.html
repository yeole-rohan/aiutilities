Frequently Asked Questions

What quality setting should I use? ▼
For web images, 70–85% quality is usually indistinguishable from 100% but saves 50–70% of the file size. For print use 90%+. Photographs tolerate lower quality than graphics with sharp edges.
Why doesn't PNG get smaller? ▼
PNG is a lossless format — the quality slider has no effect. For significant PNG compression, convert to JPG (lossy) or WebP instead. PNG is best for screenshots, logos, and graphics with transparency.
What is WebP and why is it better? ▼
WebP is a modern image format developed by Google that achieves 25–35% smaller file sizes than JPG at the same quality level. All modern browsers support it, making it ideal for web images.
