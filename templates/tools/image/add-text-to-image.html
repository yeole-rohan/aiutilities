Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add multiple lines of text? ▼
Yes. Press Enter in the text field to add line breaks. Each line is rendered separately and aligned according to your chosen alignment setting.
How do I make the text more readable on bright images? ▼
Enable the Drop Shadow option and use a dark or white color with high contrast. For very busy backgrounds, use a large font with bold enabled and a contrasting outline.
What format should I download in? ▼
Use PNG to preserve transparency and get the sharpest text. Use JPG for smaller file sizes when sharing on social media or via email.
