Frequently Asked Questions

What is HEIC? ▼
HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the default photo format used by iPhones since iOS 11. It produces smaller files than JPG at the same quality using the HEIF (High Efficiency Image Format) codec. Most non-Apple apps and older Windows software cannot open HEIC files natively.
Is my photo data private? ▼
Yes. All conversion is done entirely in your browser using the heic2any library. Your photos are never uploaded to any server.
Will EXIF data (location, date) be preserved? ▼
Browser-based HEIC conversion does not preserve EXIF metadata such as GPS location or capture date. If EXIF data is important, use a desktop application like GIMP or Adobe Lightroom.
