Frequently Asked Questions

What aspect ratio is best for social media? ▼
Instagram posts use 1:1 (square) or 4:5. Twitter/X cards are 16:9. LinkedIn posts are 1.91:1. Stories on Instagram and TikTok use 9:16 vertical format.
Will cropping reduce image quality? ▼
Cropping itself does not reduce quality — it only removes pixels outside the crop area. Choose PNG for fully lossless output, or JPG at high quality (90%+) for photos.
Can I enter exact pixel coordinates? ▼
Yes. Use the X, Y, W, H fields on the right panel to enter exact pixel values. All values are in the original image's pixel coordinates, not the display size.
